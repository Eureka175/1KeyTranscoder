Compression is always going to be better with cpu vs NVENC or Quick Sync. It will take longer, infact I attempted NVENC with h265 and it was in the 300fps range, but huge file. i.e. An episode with cpu encode of h265 takes about 45 to 50 minutes per episode of a 45minute run time. The NVENC took like 12minutes, but resulted in a much larger file size. So GPU encoding is not really beneficial if you are trying to make the best use of drive space. On another note, NVENC or Quicksync is better used for live streaming to youtube or twitch, because their servers can handle the bandwidth.
