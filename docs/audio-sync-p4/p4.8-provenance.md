## 6. Decision record

| id | subject | state after P4.8 |
|---|---|---|
| D2 | gap policy | **DEFERRED** — unchanged |
| D3 | residual acceptance | **NOT ESTABLISHED** — unchanged |
| D5 | coordinate contract | **DEFERRED** — unchanged |
| — | production measurement geometry | **UNCHANGED — `200 ms` / `100 ms`** |

P4.8 was asked whether some geometry is *reachable on real material without
destroying delay-change detection*. The measured answer is that **no single
geometry dominates**: the best geometry depends on how fast the delay moves, and
that rate is what the measurement exists to determine. A higher-coverage
configuration was available (`2000/1000` reaches apply on material where the
shipped geometry refuses) and was **not** selected, because selecting it would
have traded an 18× loss of transition resolution against a residual criterion
(D3) that is not established. The shipped geometry was therefore left alone, which
is the outcome the brief asked for when no unique answer exists.
