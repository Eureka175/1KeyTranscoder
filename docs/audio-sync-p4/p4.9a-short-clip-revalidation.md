# P4.9A — Short-Clip Fixed-Delay Revalidation

**Scope**: audio only, and only the existing **fixed integer sample offset** path.
This phase adds no algorithm, changes no threshold, and touches no production
default. Its question is whether the two short-clip corpora actually suit the
fixed-delay path — a hypothesis, tested rather than assumed.

```text
corpus      : testsets/20260903, testsets/20260904   (read-only, never modified)
branch      : research/audio-sync-p3-production-semantics
milestones  : A inventory -> B measurement -> C apply+verify -> D report
```

### 1.1 The two geometries that must not be conflated

| | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| measurement frame / hop | **200 ms / 100 ms** | `core.channel_sync.DEFAULTS` |
| TV plan window / hop | **30 s / 10 s** | `core.audio_sync_tv` |

They are different quantities with different jobs. One 200 ms frame is a delay
*observation*; one 30 s plan window is an *apply* interval. On these clips the
plan geometry needs very few windows (median **1**, max 17) while a single 30 s
measurement window would cover most of a clip — so "how many windows does this
clip have" has two different answers, and both are reported.

## 2. Fixed-delay measurement

`core.sync_estimate.estimate_pair` driven at the **production** geometry and judged
by the **production** gate, both read out of `core.channel_sync` at run time
(`frame_ms=200`, `hop_ms=100`, `search_window_ms=80`, `coarse_rate=8000`,
`min_confidence=0.3`, `min_usable_frames=5`, `min_usable_fraction=0.6`). No
threshold is typed into the harness twice, and none is lowered.

**411 cases** = 137 clips × 3 pairs (`CH3→CH1`, `CH3→CH2`, `CH3→CH4`).

| pair | cases | gate OK | gate refused | coverage p50 | coverage max | binding term |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `CH3→CH1` | 137 | **0** | 137 | 0.0 | 0.5195 | frames=107, coverage=30 |
| `CH3→CH2` | 137 | **0** | 137 | 0.0 | 0.3689 | frames=109, coverage=28 |
| `CH3→CH4` | 137 | **88** | 49 | 0.6939 | 1.0 | coverage=38, frames=11 |

```text
by status        PASS 381 / FAIL 30 / BLOCKED 0 / INSUFFICIENT_DATA 0
gate reasons     low_confidence 227, evidence_insufficient 96
```

`gate refused` is the **correct** outcome and is recorded as such, not as a
failure. Which term blocks, separated, matters more than the verdict:

* **`CH3→CH1` / `CH3→CH2` are blocked by the frame count.** On 107 / 109 clips the
  clip yields **zero** measurable frames. Confidence is not what fails — the
  `confidence ≥ 0.3` term passes on 33 / 33 of those same cases, which is the
  "signal present, peak too weak to lock" signature. Where frames do exist the
  *coverage* term takes over (30 / 28 cases), so the two terms are interlocked:
  there is never enough of the clip that is measurable.
* **`CH3→CH4`** passes on 88 clips; where it fails the binding term is coverage
  (38 cases), and only 11 fail on the frame count.

**Interpretation:** the production estimator works on this material — but the only
pair it can measure is the pair that is already aligned.

## 3. Within-clip stability

Stability *inside* a clip is tested **independently of the production estimator**:
the verifier (plain FFT cross-correlation, no GCC-PHAT, no decimation) correlates
disjoint 4 s blocks and reports the spread of the block lags. A delay that moves
shows up as a spread; a constant one does not. This is not the estimator grading
itself.

```text
cases with a qualified verifier        : 184 of 411
block-lag range over the clip          : p50 0.467 / p90 116.455 / p95 310.628 smp
block-lag MAD                          : p50 0.009 / p90   4.612 / p95   9.959 smp
cases with block range <= 2 samples    : 117
cases with block range > 10 samples    :  41
production frame spread (in samples)   : p50 4.0, max 15.0
cases needing exactly ONE plan window  : 255 of 411
frames checked per plan window         : p50 59.5, p95 97.48
```

The block-lag statistics cover **all three pairs** (they describe the material,
not eligibility). Restricted to `CH3→CH4` — the pair that can reach the gate — the
block-lag spread is at or below 2 samples on the large majority of clips, so for
that pair the delay **is** constant within a clip and a single fixed shift is a
correct description of it.

**`within_clip_stability = NOT ESTABLISHED` for `CH3→CH1` / `CH3→CH2`** — not
because the delay was found unstable, but because the measurement population is
empty: the gate refuses every clip of that pair, so there is nothing to compute a
stability statistic over. Reporting "unstable" there would be as unfounded as
reporting "stable".

## 4. Between-clip stability

| group | result |
|---|---|
| **A. same date, different clips** | `20260903` `CH3→CH4`: n=66, offset p50 **0.0**, range 3.0 samples |
| **B. different date, same pair** | `20260904` `CH3→CH4`: n=22, offset p50 **0.0**, range 2.0 samples |
| **C. same clip, different pairs** | `CH3→CH1` / `CH3→CH2` never eligible on any clip → `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` |
| **D. identity not confirmable** | the 27 non-multi-mono containers (18 with no audio at all, 9 with a single stereo stream) — **not** pooled into any statistic |

`CH3→CH4` across all 88 eligible clips: offset p50 **0.0**, min −2.0, max 1.0,
**range 3.0 samples**, MAD **0.0**, p90 1.0, p95 1.0. The per-clip offsets, with
their confidence and coverage, are in the final report §4 and in
`work/p4.9a-short/derived.json` → `between_clip` — not only the summary.

That is **stable evidence, within the scope of this rig and these two sessions**.
It is *not* evidence about wireless delay in general: the pair that carries the
off-camera feed here is unmeasurable, and neither its stability nor its
instability was established.

No new "stability threshold" is proposed, and none is used as an acceptance gate.

## 5. Actual correction and independent verification

All **136** eligible clips were run through the **whole production chain**
(`core.channel_sync.run_channel_sync`, `opts=None`, every shipped default):

```text
apply status    already_aligned 85 / measure_failed 51 / applied 0
track decisions CH3 anchor 125, CH4 already_aligned 85,
                CH1/CH2 untouched silent_track 83 each,
                CH4 untouched evidence_insufficient 37, …
```

**Zero corrections were applied, and that is the measurement result rather than a
failure.** The production path writes a shift only when a track's measured
`|delay|` exceeds `aligned_max_ms = 0.05 ms`; on this material each track is either
already aligned (`CH3`/`CH4`, offset p50 0.0 samples, range 3.0) or unmeasurable
(`CH1`/`CH2`, gate refused on every clip). There is no third possibility here.

**"It never fired" and "it works when it fires" are different claims**, and only
the second can be tested by putting a delay in on purpose. A control clip was built
from real media with a known integer delay injected into CH4 and run through the
same production chain:

```text
injected delay (TRUTH)  : 137 samples (2.854 ms)
estimated shift         : 137 samples          estimate error 0 samples
mechanics               : exact_match True, max |diff| 0.0
independent residual    : 137.119 -> 0.490 samples
```

So the fixed-delay path **measures correctly, applies the right integer shift, and
the shift is bit-exact**. The corrected PCM was compared against the f32 decoding
of the control file's own delayed track, so the comparison is decoding against
decoding and a bit-exact verdict is meaningful.

### 5.1 Residuals, split by population

| population | n | before | after |
|---|---|---|---|
| applied and independently verified (control) | 1 | 137.119 samples | **0.490** samples |
| refused by the gate — no shift | 136 | not measured | **not invented** |
| measurement cases where the verifier could not answer | 230 of 411 | — | `VERIFIER_NOT_QUALIFIED` |

Of the 175 cases where the verifier could answer, **145 agree** with the production
estimate within 2 samples and **30 disagree** — and **all 30 disagreements are on
`CH3→CH1`/`CH3→CH2`**, where the independent block lag moves by up to **533
samples inside a single clip**. A disagreement of that size is the signature of a
delay that is not constant, not of an estimator that is wrong.

The apply mechanics bound measured in P4.8 is **not** reused here as a product
residual threshold, and the 0.49-sample control residual does **not** establish D3.

## 6. Conclusion

**Fixed-delay correction is suitable for the clips where the existing path is
applicable — and on this material that means it correctly does nothing. It is not
established that these clips *need* a fixed-delay correction, because the pair that
carries the delay cannot be measured here.**

Supported by evidence:

* all 137 usable clips have the multi-mono PCM shape the path documents as
  applicable to;
* the production estimator reaches the production gate on 88 cases, all `CH3→CH4`,
  and the offset there is **0.0 samples** on both dates — already aligned;
* the whole production chain ran on 136 real clips and modified nothing, with a
  reason each time;
* a known 137-sample delay was estimated as exactly 137 and corrected bit-exactly.

Not established:

* that these clips need a correction — the delay lives on `CH3→CH1`/`CH3→CH2`,
  refused on **100 %** of clips, blocked by frame count (107 / 109 clips with zero
  measurable frames) and reported `silent_track` in 83 of 136 production runs;
* that the wireless delay here is fixed — its magnitude was never measured to a
  single number, only a per-frame delay in roughly the 950–1400 sample range that
  moves;
* that it is unstable — the clips that would show that are the ones the gate
  refuses.

**Scope limits.** Nothing here extends to C1305/C1306 or to any other wireless
system: these are two sessions on one camera body. Why `CH1`/`CH2` carry no usable
signal is **not** established (`silent_track` does not say which input feeds them
or whether the receiver was connected). Settling it needs material where the
off-camera feed is present and loud on the measured channels — more analysis of
these same files cannot produce it. Nothing here proposes a residual threshold, and
D3 remains `NOT ESTABLISHED`.
